How one sound is made

Each voice follows the path a sample took through the original Xbox's audio processor. Nothing is stored: every number below is worked out from the sound's place in the library and the world seed.

  1. Hash the nameWorld seed, then category, sound, voice and parameter slot, each hashed from the one above.
  2. Write a source sampleAn FM tone, a wavetable, a noise loop, a struck object or a pitch-drop sine, computed at 48 kHz.
  3. Store itAs 16-bit PCM, or squeezed through Xbox ADPCM and decoded again: 65 samples in 36 bytes.
  4. Play it as a voicePitch, a resonant low-pass filter and two envelopes, all updated 1,500 times a second.
  5. Place it around youThe far ear hears the voice later, quieter and duller, so it sits left, right or moves across.
  6. Mix and add reverbThree or four voices are summed and sent through a room described by decay time and damping.
